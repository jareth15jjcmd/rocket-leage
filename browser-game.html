Welcome to Turbo Arena 3D

A 3D car-soccer arena with chase cameras, nitro, a smarter bot, unlockable cars, and split-screen local multiplayer.

This is a single HTML game. Three.js is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is required to load the 3D engine.

3D Garage

Score goals to earn coins, then unlock a car color. Progress is saved in this browser.

Saved progress may not transfer to another device or private browsing.

Settings & Controls

Bot difficulty
Sound effects

Player 1 (blue): W accelerate, S reverse, A/D steer, Space nitro, Q jump/front flip.

Player 2 (orange): Arrow keys drive, Enter nitro, / jump/front flip.

Both: P pause/resume, R restart match, Escape returns to menu after pausing.
